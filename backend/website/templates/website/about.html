Quick Guide

Getting Started

  1. Set up items under Stock Management
  2. Start making sales under Sales

Cashier Accounts

  • Create cashier accounts under Manage Users
  • By default, cashiers can sell without entering a password
  • Cashiers cannot access the admin panel

Sync (Online Backup)

  • Go to Sync Settings to connect to server
  • Data syncs automatically every minute
  • To change server, disconnect first then connect to new one

Tips

  • Sales can be back-dated for missed entries
  • Partial payments are tracked automatically
  • Low stock items appear on the dashboard
  • All data is stored locally on your computer
  • Expenses can be filtered and exported by date
  • Services are tracked with payment status
